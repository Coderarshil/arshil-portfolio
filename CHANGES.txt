Resume viewer update — A4 popup + visible plane + landing/unfold

1. Resume viewer popup is now A4 proportion (210:297) instead of a wide desktop dialog.
2. The resume pages remain shown as the original high-resolution WebP renders.
3. The Galaxy flight no longer flies the paper completely out of view; the folded plane remains visible at the end of the flight.
4. Landing is now a real second animation: the visible plane returns to the center and the folds reverse one by one until the resume is flat again.
5. The Landing button changes to “Unfolding…” while the landing animation is running.
6. Parent/iframe messaging now supports START_FLIGHT, RESUME_FLIGHT_COMPLETE, LAND_RESUME, and RESUME_LANDING_COMPLETE.
7. Original PDF download remains unchanged.
